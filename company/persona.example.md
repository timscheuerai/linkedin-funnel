# Example persona rubric

At a qualified company, target the person responsible for outbound operations.
- Current remit includes revenue operations, sales operations or GTM engineering: 50 points.
- Head, director or founder with evidence of ownership: 30 points; hands-on manager: 20; other: 0.
- Evidence that the person owns outbound tooling or pipeline operations: 20 points.

Missing evidence earns no points. Ex-employees and roles outside the target company are disqualified. Threshold: 70. A relevant title alone is not proof of purchasing authority.
