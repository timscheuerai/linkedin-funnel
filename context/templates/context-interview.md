# Context interview

Capture the author's own answers and examples. Leave unanswered questions blank.

## Backstory
- Which decisions changed the direction of your life or companies?
- What failed, and what did you learn from it?
- Which stories are public, and which should stay private?

## Beliefs
- What do you believe about building, selling and running a company?
- Which common advice do you disagree with?
- What experience led you to those views?

## Audience
- Who do you want reading this?
- What do they struggle with?
- Which words do they use for that problem?

## Voice
- Which posts sound most like you?
- What do you change when someone drafts for you?
- Which expressions or writing habits feel wrong?

## Proof and material
- Which results can we cite, for which period, and from which source?
- Which interviews, talks, posts, calls or notes should go into the brain?
- Which assets and claims can be shown publicly?

## Content and conversion
- What subjects do you want to keep returning to?
- What should a reader do when they want more?
- Which company or offer belongs in this system?
- How often do you want to publish, and on which platforms?

## Visuals
- Which graphics, videos and visual references feel right?
- Which existing assets can we use?
