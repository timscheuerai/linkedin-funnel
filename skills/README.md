# All skills in LinkedIn Funnel

[Back to the template](../README.md)

The files are here in `skills/`. Open the whole template in your coding agent and ask for a skill by name, or point it directly to its `SKILL.md`. Codex and Claude Code discovery folders link to this same library.

**53 skills total:** 26 core content and branding skills, 15 content waterfall skills, 2 engagement skills, 6 additional video skills and 4 OXYGEN workflow helpers. No separate content-vault installation is needed.

## Content waterfall skills

1 idea a week becomes 1 or 2 long videos, and each video waterfalls into a newsletter, an X Article, posts and short-form on every platform. Start with [content-waterfall](content-waterfall/SKILL.md); it runs the others. Setup and the map: [the content waterfall guide](../guides/content-waterfall.md).

| Skill | Use it to |
|---|---|
| [content-waterfall](content-waterfall/SKILL.md) | Run a weekly content waterfall: find the idea of the week from what is hot on YouTube, X and LinkedIn, make 1 or 2 long videos, grow each video's 3 roots into a newsletter, an X Article, posts and short-form on every platform, ship the week and turn the engagers into conversations. Use when the author gives the idea of the week, wants posts or a newsletter from a video, hands over a recording or YouTube link, or asks to repurpose. Orchestrates the stage skills. |
| [youtube-video-research](youtube-video-research/SKILL.md) | Decide the author's next YouTube video before they record it, in 3 gates. An idea found depth-first inside their niche (a wave that is hot now, applied to their audience's outcome while the niche angle is still open), packaging proven by outliers in other niches, and the first 60 seconds scripted word for word. Ends in a go or no-go scorecard and logs every upload in a channel ledger. Use for "what should my next video be", "is this a good video idea", "validate this title or thumbnail" or "script the intro". |
| [x-article-writer](x-article-writer/SKILL.md) | Write or revise a compact native X Article in the author's voice from their newsletter, framework, posts or lead magnet, with the strongest supported result first and useful graphics placed where they explain the argument. Also writes the X post that announces a video. Use for X Articles and illustrated long posts on X; use x-copywriter or x-planner for ordinary X posts. |
| [linkedin-article-adapter](linkedin-article-adapter/SKILL.md) | Adapt a finished X Article into a native LinkedIn article while preserving its text and inline figures, recompose the cover at LinkedIn's article ratio, and prepare a numbered upload folder plus a short post to share it. Use after x-article-writer when the author wants the same article on LinkedIn. |
| [x-planner](x-planner/SKILL.md) | Plan the author's X posts together with them, day by day, from 3 lanes (LinkedIn posts repurposed for X, native posts from what is going viral in their niche, and their own launches, videos and assets). Presents a slate for the author to pick and cut, drafts the picks with x-copywriter, puts them on the board, schedules on their go and records their verdicts. Use when planning X for the week, when the author wants X posts from LinkedIn posts, trends or videos, or when the content waterfall hands over micro posts and chapter threads. |
| [linkedin-hook-writer](linkedin-hook-writer/SKILL.md) | Write, revise or compare LinkedIn hooks in the author's voice, with measured character counts and mobile-first opening budgets. Use for hook options, first-line edits, or the opening pass inside linkedin-copywriter. |
| [youtube-title-outlier-packaging](youtube-title-outlier-packaging/SKILL.md) | Find YouTube packaging outliers in the author's niche, draft 2 A/B titles from proven structures with every claim marked, and brief thumbnail concepts. Use when choosing or split-testing a YouTube title or packaging a video before recording. |
| [youtube-thumbnail-outlier-research](youtube-thumbnail-outlier-research/SKILL.md) | Research high-performing YouTube thumbnails in the author's niche, extract the patterns to borrow or avoid, and brief thumbnail concepts before rendering. Use when choosing a thumbnail direction or before youtube-thumbnail renders. |
| [longform-edit](longform-edit/SKILL.md) | Edit a long-form YouTube recording into a finished upload, usually a designed talking-head intro with proof graphics followed by a clean, clip-only screen tutorial. Use when a video needs more than a plain cut: an animated intro, numbers shown when they are said, or a few motion graphics. |
| [talking-head-video-cut](talking-head-video-cut/SKILL.md) | Cut and merge raw OBS or screen-recorded talking-head clips by selecting complete takes, trimming dead air and setup transitions, preserving the native picture, and QAing every join. Use for "remove the pauses", "cut the false starts", "just cut and merge" or a clean long-form YouTube cut. |
| [lead-magnet-delivery](lead-magnet-delivery/SKILL.md) | Turn a LinkedIn lead-magnet post ("comment KEYWORD and I'll send it") into automatic delivery on OXYGEN. Every commenter who asks gets a DM with the link and a public "Sent it over:)" reply; 2nd-degree commenters are asked to connect first; nobody is messaged twice. Use when a lead-magnet post is live and the author asks to set up delivery or send the resource to commenters. |
| [linkedin-unibox-reply](linkedin-unibox-reply/SKILL.md) | Draft LinkedIn DM replies in the author's voice for Unibox triage and 1:1 follow-ups, one at a time, and never send without the author's yes. Use to triage interested or unanswered LinkedIn threads, draft a DM reply, or rewrite a generic suggested reply into the author's voice. |
| [shortform-platform-copy](shortform-platform-copy/SKILL.md) | Write platform-specific LinkedIn, X, Instagram Reel and YouTube Shorts copy for the author's short videos from the video and their supplied facts, then put the short on their scheduler with a cover on every channel that takes one. Use when preparing, adapting, uploading or scheduling a short for several social platforms. |
| [instagram-planner](instagram-planner/SKILL.md) | Plan the author's Instagram week with them and shape each piece into a proper format. Checks that the 3 format families (talking reel, non-talking reel, carousel) are all covered, picks a sub-format per idea, writes the hook (type, wider angle, visual hook), the problem-pursuit-payoff beats, the b-roll shot list and the caption plan, and puts each pick on the content board. Use for "plan Instagram", "reels for this week", "make this a reel", "make this a carousel", "which format", "b-roll list" or "shot list". Not for the edit itself (video-editor, shorts-edit), the final platform copy and scheduling (shortform-platform-copy) or carousel rendering (graphics-designer). |
| [content-review](content-review/SKILL.md) | Review the author's published content, platform performance and production workflow, then turn the evidence into the next experiment. Use for a YouTube review, a content retrospective, why a piece underperformed, the close of a content waterfall or a review across platforms. YouTube has the detailed collection workflow; LinkedIn, X and newsletters share the review framework. For researching the next video, use youtube-video-research. |

## Engagement skills

Grow reach by commenting where the author has something real to add. Both build a queue page the author works through in 1 tap; neither posts, likes or messages on its own.

| Skill | Use it to |
|---|---|
| [linkedin-engage](linkedin-engage/SKILL.md) | Find fresh posts from the author's LinkedIn watchlist through OXYGEN's managed scraper, draft 1 or 2 natural comments each where the author has a real receipt, and publish a Copy & open queue page that learns from what they actually post. Use for a LinkedIn comment round or to grow reach through comments; it never comments, likes or messages on its own. |
| [x-engage](x-engage/SKILL.md) | Find fresh posts from the author's X watchlist through their OXYGEN X connection, draft 1 short reply each where the author has a real practice or result to add, and publish a Reply on X queue page that learns from what they actually post. Use for a daily X reply round or to grow reach through replies; it never posts, likes or follows on its own. |
