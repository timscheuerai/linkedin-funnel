# Review output

Keep the user-facing answer short enough to act on. The saved report can hold evidence tables and method detail. Scale this shape to a single piece, a channel or a whole waterfall; skip fields that do not apply.

## Verdict and coverage

Lead with the main constraint and the strongest next decision. Name the account, platform(s), goal, publication window and observation date and timezone.

| Data or content inspected | Source and date | Coverage / limit |
|---|---|---|
| Native performance | Exact export, native read or raw snapshot | Fields, window, sample size, missing permissions |
| Published content | URLs and the segments actually inspected | Full video, selected segments, transcript only or unavailable |
| Product outcomes | Attribution path and entity or cohort | Which conversions are measured, untracked or too recent |
| Production | Brief, handoff or reported time | Measured work, runtime, waiting or unknown |

Distinguish a missing log entry from missing execution. Mark stale evidence with its actual date, and label reports that rely on public counters only.

## Pieces and findings

Keep stable piece IDs and links. Show the few metrics needed to explain the decision, with their denominators and comparison window. Review losers and inconclusive pieces as well as winners. Do not collapse channel exposure, response, buyer fit and business outcomes into 1 opaque score.

For each important finding state:

- Observation and exact source; a timestamp for a content or craft claim.
- Comparison, cohort size and confidence.
- Likely interpretation and a credible alternative explanation.
- Proposed change, expected effect and the evidence that would tell the two apart.

For several platforms, show their individual findings first. Then ask where the waterfall's promise, resource, publication timing or conversion handoff breaks. Shared campaign outcomes stay shared unless piece-level attribution exists.

## Next experiment and feedback

| Experiment | Hypothesis / changed variable | Metric and comparison | Review point / decision rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| A concrete change to the next piece or workflow | What is tested and what stays comparable | Native goal metric or measured creator effort, with a real baseline | Date or evidence threshold; keep, change or inconclusive |

Name any previous experiment now resolved and what remains open. Give the next brief a directly usable instruction. If a skill change is warranted, show the proposed edit and its evidence; keep execution separate from the review unless already authorized. Name the smallest missing input that would change the verdict, not a generic request for all analytics.

## Filing

Report frontmatter follows `templates/page.md` with `type: review`. End with Source Notes linking the raw manifest, the actual exports or reads and the relevant strategy pages. File under `research/reviews/`, update the index and log, and link the applicable performance page. Do not create per-post draft or status files or a parallel planning system.
