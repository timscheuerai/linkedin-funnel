# Review the visual

Read `brand/BRAND.md`, the asset brief and only the supporting context needed to assess the work. If the brand guide is blank, review clarity and execution; do not invent brand-compliance failures.

Inspect the actual rendered artifact at the intended size and a small viewing size. With only a screenshot, distinguish visible findings from checks requiring editable source.

Assess:
- Meaning: the takeaway, reading order, truthful relationships and evidence.
- Content fit: compare the graphic with its source post or brief. Check required steps, numbers, caveats and the author's selected wording. Flag copy squeezed into tiny type and ornament that implies unsupported data.
- Identity: consistency with approved rules, where rules exist.
- Craft: text size, line breaks, alignment, spacing, contrast, clipping and connector collisions.
- Delivery: dimensions, loaded fonts, linked assets and editable/export files when supplied.

Give the few highest-impact findings with a location, effect on the reader and concrete correction. Separate functional defects, conflicts with adopted rules and subjective preferences. Prefer a concrete refinement of hierarchy, type, spacing or crop over a demand for more decoration; a deliberate, readable variation is not a brand defect. For carousels inspect every slide plus continuity; for aspect-ratio variants check the recomposed hierarchy and crop areas. Avoid numerical quality scores without a defined rubric.

Name the evidence you had: source only, prompt only, rendered but not visually inspected, or rendered and inspected. A contact sheet or a successful render does not prove every page was reviewed. When fixes are made, reinspect the changed exports before calling them resolved.

Review is report-only unless fixes were requested. For fixes, stay within the asset's scope, preserve user edits, rerender and inspect the result. Reviewing a single asset does not authorize global brand changes, Notion edits or publication.

Lead with the assessment and state inspection limits.
