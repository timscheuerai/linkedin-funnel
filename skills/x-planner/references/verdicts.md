Example rules other founders have arrived at (replace with your own):

- Every X post must stand alone without the video or the LinkedIn post. Generic derivatives of a video get cut.
- Line 1 is 1 short claim or result about the reader's world, not 2 sentences about your product.
- Lead with 1 result, not a stack of vanity numbers.
- Bullets can stay; label-colon bullets, role verbs and "Bonus:" read as AI slop.
- A lead-magnet post on X stands alone: no counts or "now on X too" from LinkedIn.
- A post the author wrote themselves goes out exactly as written; fix only a clear typo.
- The X version of a LinkedIn post keeps the hook, then goes straight to the examples; the LinkedIn argument and setup numbers come out.
- No closing engagement question; end on the last step, fact or verdict.
- A native video post is a 1 to 2 line announcement; the video carries the explanation.
- High-value threads (a full system with receipts) earn far more bookmarks than the same topic as 1 post.
- 1 piece on LinkedIn and X is 1 scheduled post with 2 channels, not 2 separate posts.
