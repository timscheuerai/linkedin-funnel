# Repurpose content

Read `AGENTS.md`, relevant identity, audience, strategy and voice pages, and the full supplied source. Use `capture-context` if the source has not been preserved. Record source sections or timestamps for each candidate.

Extract independent ideas, examples, disagreements, processes and evidence. Rank them by usefulness to the intended reader and fit with the author's strategy, not by how many posts can be produced.

For each selected piece, identify one point, its source section, pillar, funnel job and suitable format. Different wording alone does not make a second distinct piece. Preserve the source's uncertainty, time period and scope. Do not transfer a guest's experience into the author's first-person story.

Use `linkedin-copywriter` for LinkedIn drafts and `graphics-designer` or `flowchart` when the relationship is clearer visually. A request for a video brief can produce a sourced storyboard and shot list; do not claim a rendered video without actually rendering and inspecting it.

Keep the CTA aligned with the current offer. Mention the original source when it helps the reader, rather than opening every piece with an announcement about repurposing.

Deliver the requested quantity supported by the source; explain any shortfall. Include a compact source-to-piece map outside the copy. Save to Notion only within the requested scope using the configured destination and existing-card check.
