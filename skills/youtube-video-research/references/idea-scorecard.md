# Idea scorecard

5 criteria, 0 to 2 points each. Pass: 8 or more of 10 and no 0 on Heat, Gap or Fit. This is a working rule, not a law: backtest it on the author's last uploads when they have some, and recalibrate it with every new row in `strategy/youtube-channel-ledger.md`.

| Criterion | 2 | 1 | 0 | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Heat**: is the wave hot now? | 3 or more outliers (3x or more) on the wave from 2 or more channels, the newest under 14 days old | outliers only 15 to 30 days old, or from 1 channel | no wave: an evergreen topic or no outliers | heat table, top outliers |
| **Gap**: is the niche angle still open? | no niche video on the wave has outlier numbers, and few exist | niche videos exist, many at baseline or small, none an outlier | a bigger channel in the niche already has an outlier on this angle | probes, the per-wave list from gate 1 step 4 |
| **Fit**: is the outcome the author's niche? | the outcome the audience pays for, said in the title | adjacent: a related output the audience cares about less | another audience, personal growth or general news | the title draft, `audience/ideal-follower.md` |
| **Timing**: can it ship in the window? | goes live within 14 days of the wave's start or its latest jump | 15 to 30 days | later | wave date at its source, the release plan |
| **Receipt**: can the author show it? | a real run on screen and a cleared number | a real run, no number | opinion or explainer only | `identity/proof.md` |

## Notes

- Heat comes from outside the author's channel, Fit from inside it. A personal-brand or building-in-public idea passes only when its outcome is the niche outcome, not reach for its own sake.
- Gap and Timing usually separate a hit from a miss on the same wave: a few days late, and a bigger channel in the niche has already taken the angle.
- An empty probe on a dead wave is not a gap. Gap counts only with Heat 1 or 2.
- 2 waves in 1 idea can work, for example a creative tool and a new model combined for the niche outcome.
- Overlap: if the idea repeats the angle of 1 of the author's last 3 videos, say so in the slate. A sequel to a hit is allowed; a rerun is not.
- Receipt 2 needs the number cleared in `identity/proof.md` before recording, not after.

## Slate row format

```text
<wave> for <niche outcome>   Heat 2 · Gap 1 · Fit 2 · Timing 1 · Receipt 2 = 8, pass
Heat: <3 outlier URLs with ratio, channel, age>
Gap: <probe row or niche list: n videos, best ratio, who>
Timing: wave started <date> (<source>), live by <date> = <n> days
Receipt: <what the author shows; which proof row>
Overlap: <none, or which recent video>
```
