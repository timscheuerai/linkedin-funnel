# Anti-patterns in DM replies

Generic suggested replies tend to fall into these shapes. Do not copy them; rewrite from the thread in the author's own words.

1. **Walk-you-through and demo:** "Would you like me to walk you through how it works or set up a quick demo?"
2. **Grab 15 minutes:** "Happy to walk you through the latest features. Want to schedule a quick demo this week?"
3. **Dive deeper call:** "Let's set up a quick call to dive deeper."
4. **Happy to hop on:** "Happy to share more details or hop on a quick call. Just let me know what works best for you!"
5. **Book a time after a soft message:** "Let me book a time shortly."
6. **Scale-teams closer:** "Always happy to share how we help teams like yours scale that further."
7. **Book whenever:** "Feel free to book a time here whenever you're ready."
8. **An estimate that needs a call:** "Happy to give a rough estimate if we connect. Could we hop on a quick call?"
9. **Content dump offer:** "I'd be happy to share more of our content. Let me know what areas interest you most."
10. **Long catch-up status:** several status updates, a booking link and a reciprocal product try in 1 message. Keep social replies to 2 to 4 short lines.
11. **Jargon product summary on a signup:** a list of product layers instead of 1 plain line and 1 question.
12. **Loose numbered dump:** breezy numbered steps without a clear question at the end.
13. **Invented numbers:** prices or volumes the author never stated for this thread.
14. **Em dashes** anywhere in DM copy.

A booking link is fine when the person asked for a call or a call is already being arranged. The failure is the default sales closer, not every call.
