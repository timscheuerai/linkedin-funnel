# YouTube thumbnail outlier research

Run before a new thumbnail render. Pair with `youtube-title-outlier-packaging` for the titles and `youtube-thumbnail` for the pixels.

## Research

1. Find the outliers: the vidIQ plugin (outlier search and visually similar thumbnails) if installed, otherwise niche searches and the channels the audience watches. Use the outlier definition in `youtube-title-outlier-packaging`.
2. Download the full-size thumbnails (`https://i.ytimg.com/vi/<id>/maxresdefault.jpg`) of the top references and look at them at full size and at 320 x 180.
3. Sort the patterns: overused in the niche, and rare but working.
4. Write 4 to 6 concepts with the on-thumbnail text, the layout, the title they pair with and any number that still needs proof.

## Patterns that repeat in AI, GTM and lead-gen niches

Often overused, so do not clone them blindly:

- Big face + giant 3D numeral + short boast
- Face + an army of product logos + "Steal these"
- "UNLIMITED LEADS" + spreadsheet banners
- Rows of 3 tool logos or "vs" frames

Usually still useful:

- 1 large product mark instead of a row of small ones
- A small countable badge, only when the number is proven
- A real scene or setup of the author at work instead of a cut-out face
- A calm expression over a shocked open mouth

## Rules

- Use real photos of the author; never generate or "polish" their face.
- Little text: about 2 to 4 words.
- A number on the thumbnail must be shown in the video.
- Differentiate with the scene, the product mark and the hook wording.
- Check every concept at 1280 x 720 and 320 x 180.

## Hand-off

Return the references with URLs, the patterns to borrow and avoid, and 4 to 6 concepts ready for `youtube-thumbnail`. The author picks before anything is rendered.
