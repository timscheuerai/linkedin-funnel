# Worked example: description and share post

A synthetic example for an article about a founder's LinkedIn content system. Every number below is a placeholder; replace it only with figures cleared in `identity/proof.md`, with their time windows.

## Short article description

LinkedIn brought us our first <N> signups. I break down the system behind it: how I capture my thinking, how 1 idea becomes several pieces, the resources I give away, and how we turn relevant engagement into qualified conversations.

## Post to share the article

LinkedIn brought us our first <N> signups.

A year ago I posted 5 times a week and still struggled to get leads.

I let AI draft from too little context, hit publish and hoped the right people would find us.

After 3 iterations, the workflow looks different.

I give the AI my actual thinking: weekly voice notes, customer calls, previous posts and the topics I care about. Skills help with the repeatable production work, but I still edit every piece. Then we look at who engages, check whether they fit, and follow up where there is a real reason to talk.

I put the system and the diagrams into this article. If you're building founder-led distribution, I hope it helps you build your own version.

## Why it is built this way

- The first line is the result, in the author's words, with a cleared number.
- The middle is a before and after with the mechanism, not a list of tips.
- The post gives a reason to open the article and does not repeat the article's steps.
- A time-window qualifier stays attached to the number it belongs to.
- The link goes in the first comment or the article card, as the author prefers; the post says where.
