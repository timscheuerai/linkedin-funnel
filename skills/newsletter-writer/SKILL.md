# Write one issue

Read `context/voice/`, audience, strategy and the full supplied sources. Establish the newsletter's promise and the issue's one useful idea. Existing editions define recurring format; do not invent a permanent house style from one draft.

Outline an opening observation, the explanation or worked example, practical application and a relevant next step. Use sections only when they help reading. Attribute guest experience and distinguish opinion from evidence. Every metric needs its population, period and source.

Draft a subject and preview line whose promise the issue delivers. Do not use unsupported urgency or turn the preview into a repeated subject. Write links with descriptive labels; verify targets when supplied or retrieved.

Edit for new information per paragraph. Preserve the author's distinctive phrasing; remove inflated transitions and generic lessons. Label any missing quote or fact outside the copy.

Deliver subject, preview line, paste-ready body and a short source/check list outside it. When requested, use `repurpose-content` to extract distinct posts. A completed issue is a draft until the requested publishing action actually succeeds.
