# Build a launch video

Read the product brief, real feature behavior and brand context. Establish audience, runtime, aspect ratio, key message and output. This skill provides a production procedure; a motion renderer, audio provider and paid generation are separate prerequisites.

Create a beat sheet with spoken line, visual action, evidence/asset and target duration. Every screen must represent a real feature or be labeled concept/demo. Keep an editable script beside the render project.

Use the available renderer and inspect its current APIs before implementation. Align visuals to measured narration/word cues. Keep narration text and beat timing in one source so revisions do not leave scenes out of sync.

Choose the user's intended voice and licensed music. Paid generation needs the requested scope and budget; do not silently select a recurring service or imitate a real person's voice without the appropriate basis.

Render a short representative segment, inspect typography, timing and audio, then render the whole artifact. Keep output files separate from source assets. Use `shorts-qa` for applicable checks and verify the final codec/playback requirements.

Deliver video, editable project, script, asset credits and validation notes. No prebuilt Remotion scene library or automatic uploader is implied by this skill.
