# Golden comments

The comments the author actually posted or took from the LinkedIn Comment Queue, with their edits, newest at the bottom. The drafting reads them as the best examples of the author's comment voice. `scripts/page_feedback.py --append-golden` appends to this file; add posted comments from the fetch's `feedback` block by hand (" / " marks a line break).

Format: `- <date>, <post author> (<move>, edited or as drafted): "<final text>"`
