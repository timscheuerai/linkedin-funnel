# Personal bank

Moments from the author's life that they have already told in public, in the size of a comment, each with the page it comes from. `scripts/personal_refs.py` reads the table under "Stories" and matches rows to each picked post by topic.

> Status: empty template. Fill it in setup step 2 ([setup.md](setup.md)) with 15 to 30 moments from the author's own published posts and `context/identity/` pages. Only moments the author has made public; a private one needs their yes first.

## How to use a story

- **Only when it is the direct parallel.** The post is about a startup dying from no distribution, and the author's did. Not: the post is about outbound, so mention a hobby. A story that needs a bridge sentence is the wrong story.
- **2 to 4 of 10 comments carry a story.** The rest react, add know-how, answer the post's question or ask one.
- **Say it in passing**, the way the author would mention it over a coffee, in brackets if it fits. Only with the details the source gives.
- **Rotate.** A story used in the last 7 days waits; `personal_refs.py` shows when each was last used.
